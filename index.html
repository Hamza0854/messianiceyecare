Our services

Everything you need for healthy eyes, under one roof. Select a service to learn more.

  • A thorough assessment of your vision and eye health using modern diagnostic equipment, so small problems are caught before they affect your sight.

  • Accurate prescriptions tailored to your individual visual needs, whether for reading, distance, computer work or all three.

When to have your eyes checked

Glaucoma is common in Ghana and often has no early symptoms. Regular checks protect sight that cannot be restored once it is lost.

Book an eye examination
  • Blurred or cloudy vision at distance or when reading
  • Frequent headaches after reading or screen work
  • Red, itchy, watery or painful eyes that do not settle
  • Halos or glare around lights at night
  • A child who squints, sits close to the TV or struggles with the board
  • Diabetes, high blood pressure or a family history of glaucoma

Good sight helps children learn

Many children do not know that what they see is blurred. We screen vision gently, check how both eyes work together, and prescribe spectacles when they are needed, so your child can read, play and learn with confidence.

  • Screening before starting school, and yearly after that
  • Checks for squint and lazy eye
  • Child-friendly frames that stand up to play
